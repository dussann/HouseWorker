# FixHome - Project Vision

## Version

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

**Last Updated:** August 2026

---

# 1. Vision

FixHome is a digital marketplace that connects homeowners with verified craftsmen for home maintenance and repair services.

The goal is to simplify the process of finding reliable professionals, requesting home services, comparing offers, communicating with craftsmen, and managing service requests from a single application.

The initial launch will target the Serbian market, focusing on Belgrade and Novi Sad, with a long-term vision of expanding throughout the Balkans.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Finding trustworthy craftsmen is often difficult.

Customers usually rely on:

* personal recommendations
* social media groups
* classified ads
* random internet searches

These approaches create several problems:

* difficult to verify craftsmen
* unclear pricing
* slow communication
* no centralized request management
* no service history
* limited customer reviews

Craftsmen also face challenges:

* difficulty finding new customers
* inconsistent workload
* lack of professional online presence
* inefficient communication

FixHome aims to solve these problems by providing a centralized marketplace.

---

# 3. Product Goals

The primary goals of the application are:

* Connect customers with verified craftsmen.
* Simplify the process of requesting home services.
* Allow craftsmen to compete by sending offers.
* Increase customer trust through ratings and reviews.
* Provide transparent communication between both parties.
* Build a scalable platform that can support multiple cities and countries.

---

# 4. Target Users

## Customer

People who need home repair or maintenance services.

Typical examples include:

* leaking pipes
* electrical failures
* painting
* appliance repair
* air conditioning maintenance
* locksmith services
* carpentry

---

## Craftsman

Professionals who provide home services.

Examples:

* electrician
* plumber
* painter
* carpenter
* locksmith
* HVAC technician
* appliance repair technician

---

## Administrator

Responsible for managing the platform.

Responsibilities include:

* user management
* craftsman verification
* category management
* platform moderation

---

# 5. Core Features

## Customer Features

* User registration
* Login
* Customer profile
* Create service requests
* Upload images
* Select category
* Specify service location
* Receive multiple offers
* Accept one offer
* Chat with craftsman
* Track service progress
* Leave reviews

---

## Craftsman Features

* Registration
* Professional profile
* Service categories
* Receive matching requests
* Submit offers
* Chat with customers
* Manage active jobs
* View ratings and reviews

---

## Administrator Features

* Manage users
* Verify craftsmen
* Manage categories
* Monitor platform activity

---

# 6. Business Model

FixHome is designed as a marketplace platform.

Potential monetization options include:

* commission per completed service
* premium craftsman subscriptions
* promoted craftsmen
* featured listings
* advertising partnerships

The initial MVP focuses on validating the marketplace before introducing monetization.

---

# 7. MVP Scope

The first version of the application should include:

* Authentication
* Customer profiles
* Craftsman profiles
* Service categories
* Service requests
* Image upload
* Offer management
* Reviews
* Basic administration

The MVP should validate the core marketplace workflow before adding advanced features.

---

# 8. Future Features

Future releases may include:

* real-time chat
* push notifications
* integrated payments
* GPS navigation
* nearby craftsman search
* appointment scheduling
* recurring maintenance reminders
* AI-assisted request categorization
* document and invoice management
* multilingual support
* multi-country deployment

---

# 9. Non-Functional Requirements

The application should be:

* secure
* responsive
* scalable
* maintainable
* modular
* production-ready

The architecture should support future migration from an MVP to a large-scale distributed platform.

---

# 10. Success Metrics

The product will be considered successful if it can:

* simplify the process of finding craftsmen
* reduce response time for service requests
* increase transparency through offers and reviews
* provide a positive user experience
* support future growth without major architectural changes

---

# 11. Long-Term Vision

FixHome should become the leading digital platform for home services in the Balkan region.

The platform should be recognized for:

* trusted craftsmen
* transparent pricing
* fast communication
* excellent user experience
* reliable service management
* scalable architecture suitable for continuous growth
